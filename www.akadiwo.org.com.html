About Akadiwo

A faith-driven non-profit working for community empowerment, economic stability and spiritual renewal in Uganda.

Who we are

Akadiwo is a faith-driven non-profit organization operating in Uganda. By linking biblical discipleship with sustainable development, we serve vulnerable communities to restore dignity, cultivate self-reliance and build lasting generational hope.

At a glance

  • 100% free access programs
  • 3 pillars in one integrated model
  • Based in Uganda, East Africa

Our programs

Three pillars that work together. Every program is free to the families we serve.

💡 Free Christian Education

  • Tuition-free access: removing financial barriers for low-income children.
  • Biblical discipleship: integrating faith, ethics and character building.
  • Life skills & leadership: mentoring youth for sustainable career pathways.

🌍 Family Support & Livelihoods

  • Household care: emergency food security and relief.
  • Faith coaching: family counseling rooted in biblical values.
  • Economic growth: financial literacy and micro-enterprise training.

🌱 Climate Action

  • Climate-smart farming: teaching sustainable soil and crop techniques.
  • Creation care: mobilizing youth for tree planting and conservation.
  • Food security: establishing resilient home vegetable gardens.
